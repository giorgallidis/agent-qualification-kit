# Facts sheet (template): the ONLY facts an output may state as true without [verify]

Fill this in before writing any task card. Every claim an output makes that isn't here or
in a source file the task card names must be marked [verify] / [unverified] / [placeholder].
This is what the judge checks claims against — a thin facts sheet makes a weak judge; keep
every number sourced (where did it come from, and when).

## Who / what
- <The organization, product, or role this process serves. One or two lines, factual,
  no marketing language.>
- <Who the target audience / customer / recipient is.>

## Numbers (every number sourced, range shown where there is one)
- <e.g. "Median cost of the stack this product replaces: $X/month across N tools
  (source, date read)." Not a picked number — the median of real comps, cited.>

## Never public / never say
- <Anything that must never appear in public or recipient-facing copy: prices that are
  negotiated case by case, internal metrics, unnamed-consent client names, deal terms,
  anything the facts sheet owner has not approved for release.>

## Banned claims
- <Specific phrases or claim types that are off-limits regardless of whether they're
  technically defensible — e.g. unsubstantiated percentage claims, compliance claims you
  haven't verified, any testimonial that isn't on file.>

## Decisions that are not yours to make
- <Pricing, discounts, legal terms, anything that needs a named human's sign-off. An
  output that states one of these as decided, rather than pending, is a policy violation.>

## Standing placeholders
- <Things every output in this process should leave blank rather than invent — a demo
  link, a mailing address, a date not yet set.>
